# Spike LR

A man-portable anti-tank guided missile with a camera seeker that you steer and lock in flight.

| Item | Classname |
| --- | --- |
| **Spike LR** launcher | `itc_land_spikeLR` |
| **Spike LR** missile | `itc_land_spikeLR_1rnd` |

## Firing

1. Select the Spike LR and aim at the target area. The sight shows the seeker view (**DTV** daylight or **HOT** thermal).
2. Fire. The missile flies towards the point you aimed at, and you look through its camera.
3. Steer the camera with the aim keys (mouse or **Aim Up/Down/Left/Right**). How fast it turns is the **Camera sensitivity** [setting](../usage.md#settings) (per player).
4. Lock a target with the **Lock Target** key (or the **Auto Elevation** key, `gunElevAuto`): the missile then flies to it on its own. Without a lock (**MANUAL**), it follows the camera.

The camera closes when the missile hits, when you switch weapons or when you die.
