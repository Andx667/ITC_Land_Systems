# ITC Land Systems [BETA]

**Beta status:** ITC Land Systems is in beta. Features are still being tested and balanced, and you may run into bugs or unfinished content. Please report problems on the issue tracker (see Source & Issues below).

**ITC Land Systems** (ITC) adds and improves Arma 3's land-based weapon systems: artillery with real fire control, air and missile defence, counter-battery radar, active protection, guided munitions and UAVs.

# Requirements

- [CBA_A3](https://github.com/CBATeam/CBA_A3)
- [ACE3](https://github.com/acemod/ACE3)

# Features

- **Artillery** — M4 Sholef 2 and 2S9 Sochor 2 howitzers, Mk45 Hammer 2 naval gun, M5 Seara 2 MLRS and Zamak MRLi, with HE, ICM, smoke, illumination, mines, laser- and GPS-guided rounds and submunition rounds.
- **Ammunition handler** — choose round, charge, fuze and guidance, then follow the loading sequence; the status is shared with the whole crew.
- **Fire control tablet** — firing solutions from ballistic tables, a battery computer for multi-gun fire missions, and stored target locations.
- **Air and missile defence** — CIWS that shoot down incoming artillery, mortar and rocket rounds, MIM-145 Defender 2 and S750 Rhea 2 SAM sites, and a Vertical Launch System with a target dialog.
- **COBRA counter-battery radar** — detects incoming shells, predicts impacts, back-traces firing positions and sounds alarm sirens.
- **Active Protection System** — intercepts incoming rockets and missiles.
- **Spike LR** — man-portable ATGM with a steerable camera seeker.
- **UAVs and recon** — Darter 2, Greyhawk, Ababil-3 and Sentinel 2 UAVs, a carryable AL-6 bag, packable UAVs and laser designator, and a ROVER tablet for aircraft video feeds.
- **CBA settings** — toggle CIWS and APS, tune COBRA range and engagement window, and set Spike camera sensitivity.
