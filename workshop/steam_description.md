# ITC Land Systems Redux [BETA]

**Beta status:** ITC Land Systems Redux is in beta. Features are still being tested and balanced, and you may run into bugs or unfinished content. Please report problems on the issue tracker (see Source & Issues below).

**ITC Land Systems Redux** (ITC) is about the artillery fight in Arma 3: finding targets, putting rounds on them, and detecting and stopping the enemy's incoming fire. Made for player crews.

# Requirements

- [CBA_A3](https://github.com/CBATeam/CBA_A3)
- [ACE3](https://github.com/acemod/ACE3)

# Features

## Observation

- **UAVs** — Darter 2 and Tayran 2, Greyhawk and Ababil-3, Sentinel 2, and the AL-6 as a carryable UAV bag.
- **ROVER tablet** — the video feed of laser-designating aircraft.
- **Strider RV** — recon vehicle (unarmed, HMG, GMG) with ITC optics readouts.
- **Packable equipment** — pack and unpack UAVs and the Remote GLTD laser designator.

## Artillery and fire control

- **Guns and rockets** — M4 Sholef 2 and 2S9 Sochor 2 howitzers, Mk45 Hammer 2 naval gun, M5 Seara 2 MLRS and Zamak MRLi, and the MN230 Vertical Launch System with cruise missiles.
- **Munitions** — HE, ICM, smoke, illumination, mines, laser- and GPS-guided rounds and submunition rounds, with proximity, time and delay fuzes.
- **Ammunition handler** — choose round, charge, fuze and guidance, then follow the automatic or semi-automatic loading sequence, or prepare and drop each round by hand on a mortar; the status is shared with the whole crew.
- **Fire control tablet** — exact firing solutions for every charge, low and high angle, with the time fuze setting, a battery computer (BCS) for planning and adjusting multi-gun fire missions, and stored target locations; the tablet and the ammunition handler run as web pages in the game's browser (Arma 3 2.20+), with the classic interfaces one click away.
- **Datalink** — the FDC sends firing data and fire orders to linked guns and rocket launchers, observers hand targets from UAV, GLTD and Strider optics to the FDC, and COBRA engagements become counter-fire missions.
- **Gunner sights** — azimuth, quadrant, solution and fire order readouts with an alignment guide on the crosshair; Russian guns read in 6000 mils.

## Counter-artillery

- **COBRA counter-battery radar** — detects incoming shells, predicts their impact, back-traces the firing position and sounds alarm sirens near predicted impacts.
- **CIWS** — Mk49 Spartan 2 and Praetorian 2 shoot down incoming artillery, mortar and rocket rounds.
- **SAM sites** — MIM-145 Defender 2 and S750 Rhea 2 with proximity-fuzed missiles, and the MN230 air defence launcher.

## Compatibility

Loaded only when the other mod is loaded too:

- **RHS: AFRF / USAF** — ITC versions of the 2S1, 2S3 and M109A6 with ammunition handler and mounted tablet, ITC sights and firing tables for the D-30 and M119A2, BCS firing tables for the RHS mortars.
- **CUP** — ITC versions of the D-30, M119, M270 MLRS and the M1129 Stryker mortar carrier (a hand-loaded 120 mm mortar).
- **BWMod** — ITC versions of the Panzerhaubitze 2000, BCS firing tables for the MRS120 mortar.
- **GTK Boxer** — ITC versions of the Boxer RCH 155.

## Also included

Active Protection System: intercepts incoming rockets and missiles.

## Settings

CBA settings to toggle CIWS and APS, tune the COBRA detection range and engagement window, switch between the HTML and the classic tablet, and show Russian guns in 6000 or NATO 6400 mils.
