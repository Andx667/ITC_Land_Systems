# CUP Vehicles Compatibility

`itc_land_compat_cup_vehicles`: Loads only with CUP Vehicles. ITC versions of the M270 MLRS (ITC 230 mm rockets, MLRS sight and mounted tablet, like the Seara 2) and of the M1129 Stryker mortar carrier (ITC 120 mm mortar rounds, ITC sight, firing tables and BCS battery type).

## Dependencies

- ITC: `itc_land_common`, `itc_land_ballistics`, `itc_land_veh_sights`, `itc_land_veh_weapons`
- ACE: `ace_interaction`
- Other mods: `CUP_Weapons_Ammunition`, `CUP_Weapons_VehicleWeapons`, `CUP_TrackedVehicles_M270`, `CUP_WheeledVehicles_Stryker`
